Source formatting Source formatting affects chunkers differently. Text that is hard-wrapped at a fixed line width contains line breaks in the middle of sentences. A recursive chunker that tries line breaks before sentence boundaries will then split at arbitrary line positions, which behaves much like fixed-size chunking. Normalizing single line breaks into spaces before chunking, or placing the sentence separator before the line separator, avoids this problem.

Recursive chunking tries larger structural boundaries before smaller ones. A typical hierarchy tries paragraph breaks first, then line breaks, then sentence boundaries, and finally word boundaries. Source formatting Source formatting affects chunkers differently. Text that is hard-wrapped at a fixed line width contains line breaks in the middle of sentences. A recursive chunker that tries line breaks before sentence boundaries will then split at arbitrary line positions, which behaves much like fixed-size chunking. Normalizing single line breaks into spaces before chunking, or placing the sentence separator before the line separator, avoids this problem.
