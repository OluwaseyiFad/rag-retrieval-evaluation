Project Orchard delivery review — June 10, 2026

Pinebridge's connector harness arrived fourteen days late. Project Orchard moved its pilot from June 12 to June 26. The readiness board had gone green after a demonstration video, before a runnable harness was available.

While waiting, the project used a small static fixture. It contained only one page of records and concealed a pagination boundary. The revised acceptance procedure requires a representative multi-page fixture and an executable harness link before readiness can turn green. Priya Nair requested a revised supplier delivery plan, and the team reserved an additional validation window.

The slip is attributed to the harness delivery. The demonstration video was useful for training but did not satisfy the contracted artifact requirement or prove readiness at production volume.
