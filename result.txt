Source formatting Source formatting affects chunkers differently. Text that is hard-wrapped at a fixed line width contains line breaks in the middle of sentences. A recursive chunker that tries line breaks before sentence boundaries will then split at arbitrary line positions, which behaves much like fixed-size chunking.
