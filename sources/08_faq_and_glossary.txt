AtlasDesk FAQ and Glossary

Frequently asked questions

Q: Can I edit an invoice after it is finalized?
A: No. Corrections to a finalized invoice appear as adjustment entries on a later invoice.

Q: Does GuideFlow use Draft articles?
A: No. Only Approved articles are used for customer-facing answers.

Q: Why is a deleted user still on my invoice?
A: Seats are billed from the daily billing snapshot. If the user was active when the snapshot ran, the seat is billed for that day even if it was deactivated afterwards.

Q: Do automation credits expire?
A: On Starter and Growth, unused automation credits expire at the end of the billing period.

Q: Can an agent see audit logs?
A: No. Audit logs are visible only to administrators and to users with the Compliance permission.

Q: Does changing my payment card move my payment deadline?
A: No. Neither finalization nor the payment deadline moves when the payment method changes.

Q: How long does a region migration take?
A: The maintenance window lasts up to six hours. Attachments may show as pending migration for up to 24 hours afterwards.

Q: Why did GuideFlow send a question to an agent instead of answering?
A: No eligible passage cleared the minimum relevance threshold, or the answer had no citation.

Glossary

Hold (ticket): The On Hold ticket status, used while waiting for an internal team or a third party. It pauses the resolution timer.
Hold (legal): A legal hold, which suspends deletion of covered records until it is released.
Credit (automation): A unit of background-job usage. Automation credits have no monetary value.
Credit (account): A monetary amount that reduces the total due on a future invoice.
Credit (service): A contractual adjustment for platform availability below the agreed level.
Snapshot (billing): The daily record of active seats, taken at 23:30 in the workspace billing time zone.
Snapshot (index): The frozen copy of knowledge passages that GuideFlow searches, created after each indexing run.
Retry (payment): A repeated attempt to charge a card after a failed payment.
Retry (webhook): A repeated attempt to deliver an event to an integration endpoint.
Review (article): The publication state in which an article waits for supervisor approval.
Review (service): A process triggered by repeated support SLA breaches.
Passage: The retrievable unit of a knowledge article.
Seat: A licensed support user.
Light seat: A reduced-price seat that can read tickets and knowledge but cannot reply to customers.
Incident commander: The person who coordinates an incident and assigns owners.
