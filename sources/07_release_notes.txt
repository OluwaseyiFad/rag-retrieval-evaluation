AtlasDesk Release Notes

These notes describe changes to AtlasDesk in reverse chronological
order. When a note describes a value that was later changed, the most
recent note is authoritative. Older notes are kept for reference and may
describe behavior that no longer applies.

Release 4.2 (current)

- Audit-log retention remains 180 days. Extended audit retention of 2
  years is now available as an Enterprise add-on.
- The default GuideFlow minimum relevance threshold was raised from 0.50
  to 0.55.
- Stale knowledge passages now receive a ranking penalty of 0.05 instead
  of being excluded from customer-facing retrieval.
- The maximum temporary access grant was reduced from 90 days to 30
  days.

Release 4.0

- Audit-log retention was increased from 90 days to 180 days. Records
  that had already been deleted under the old 90-day rule were not
  restored.
- Failed payment recovery changed from two attempts to three attempts.
  The retry interval before the third attempt is 48 hours.
- Light seats were introduced for users who only need to read tickets
  and knowledge.

Release 3.6

- The GuideFlow answer generation limit increased from two passages to
  three passages.
- Stale knowledge passages are excluded from customer-facing retrieval.
  This behavior was replaced in release 4.2.
- Webhook deliveries are retried up to five times before being moved to
  the dead-letter list.

Release 3.2

- Cross-language retrieval was introduced as an optional workspace
  setting.
- The payment deadline for Growth workspaces was extended from 10 days
  to 15 days after finalization.

Known issues

- In workspaces that migrated regions before release 4.0, some audit
  records from the migration window show the old region in the target
  object field. The records are otherwise correct.
- The Billing History page shows snapshot times in the viewer's browser
  time zone instead of the workspace billing time zone. The snapshot
  itself still runs at 23:30 in the billing time zone.
